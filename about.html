company story

PrintShopKarachi, formerly known as StrokeArt & PrintShopKarachi, stands as one of Pakistan's most seasoned and trusted designing & printing shops. Print Shop Karachi offers a diverse range of high-quality online printing services, ensuring top-notch quality and satisfaction. Our services include Business Cards, Visiting Cards, Brochure Printing, Catalogue Printing, Flyer Printing, Envelopes Printing, Stickers Printing, Stationery Printing, Letterhead & Letter pads Printing, Annual Reports Printing, Appointment Cards, Bifold Brochures, Billboards, Billbooks, Books Printing, Boxes Printing, Car & Truck Wraps, Vehicle Wraps, Carbonized Books, Cash Memo Booklets, CD/DVD Covers, Certificates, Company Profile Printing, Desk Calendars, Die Cut Cards, Double Sided Cards, Double Sided Flyers, Eid Cards, Exhibition Printing, Folded Cards, Horizontal, Invitation Cards, Invoice Booklets, Labels Printing, Large Envelopes, Leaflets Printing, Letterheads Printing, Letterpads Printing, Menu Cards, Packages Printing, Pamphlets Printing, Panaflex, Picture Cards, Pocket Calendars, Post Cards, Posters, Presentation Folders, Rollup or X Stands, Rounded Corner Cards, Shop Signs, Single Sided Brochures, Single Sided Flyers, Small Envelopes, Special Shaped Cards, Spot UV Cards, Transparent Cards, Tri Fold Brochures, Vertical Cards, Visiting Cards, Voucher Booklets, Wall Calendars, Wallpapers & much more. With a legacy of excellence and a commitment to innovation, PrintShopKarachi is your ultimate destination for all your designing & printing needs.

History

Established on August 14, 1975, as StrokeArt, BSU Prints quickly gained recognition for its exceptional services, including Business Cards, Visiting Cards, Stationery, Brochures, and more. In March 23, 2000, StrokeArt underwent a transformation and emerged as PrintShopKarachi, expanding its services to include Catalogue, Company Profile printing, and additional offerings.
